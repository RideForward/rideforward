Operator: Global Connection (Pvt) Ltd (“RideForward”, “we”, “us” or “our”)

This Policy explains how RideForward collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit the website or submit an initial enquiry, screening request or opportunity application. We aim to handle information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Sri Lankan law and data protection requirements.

1. Information collected

The current initial enquiry form asks for your name, telephone number, district, occupation, reason for contacting RideForward and any additional information you choose to provide. We may also collect membership and opportunity records, communications, payment status if you later become a member, and technical or security logs. Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless a programme specifically requests it and explains why.

2. How information is used

We use information to receive and review enquiries and applications; contact people about screening, eligibility and outcomes; discuss possible membership options; administer confirmed memberships and partner opportunities; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain and improve the website; and meet legal, accounting, recordkeeping and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing with participating partners

We may share relevant information with donors, sponsors and participating vendors or partners when reasonably needed to assess or administer an opportunity or discuss a proposed membership offer. We aim to share only information reasonably necessary for that purpose. Application information is not provided to partners for unrelated marketing merely because it was submitted to RideForward. Any additional use must have an appropriate legal basis and consent where required.

4. Google Sheets and service providers

The website form currently sends the submitted fields to a Google Apps Script endpoint connected to a Google Sheet. Google and other providers may process information under their own service terms and privacy policies. Access to records should be limited to authorised people who need it. The operator should maintain appropriate account security, access controls and backups.

5. Cookies and local storage

The launch countdown uses the visitor’s browser clock to calculate the time remaining to the published launch date. The current homepage does not need to store a personal countdown deadline in browser storage. If analytics, advertising or additional tracking is introduced, this Policy should be updated and required consent obtained.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Notify us if you believe your information has been misused.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for enquiry review, membership administration, opportunity fulfilment, legal obligations, disputes and legitimate operational purposes. Periods may vary by record type. We review whether information remains necessary and securely dispose of or anonymise it when appropriate and lawful.

8. Your choices and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, ask how it is used, or raise an objection or complaint. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security or dispute purposes. We may verify identity before responding. Contact RideForward using the telephone or company website details below.

9. Children

RideForward is generally intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect children’s information through adult membership or screening forms. A programme permitting minors must have a suitable process, including legally appropriate parent or guardian consent.

10. International processing

Providers or partners may process information outside Sri Lanka. RideForward will take steps required by applicable law and use appropriate safeguards where required.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy when services, partners, technology or legal obligations change. The revised version and effective date will be posted here. Material changes may also be communicated directly where appropriate.
