A partnership-led model

Membership should work for people—and partners.

We do not want a one-price-fits-everyone approach. After an initial conversation, RideForward will work with the participating vendor or company to understand the available benefits, the member’s financial circumstances and a contribution that is realistic and sustainable. The proposed amount and benefits will be explained before anyone decides to join.

How it works

A straightforward way to get started.

STEP 01

Introduce yourself

Complete the initial form and tell us what opportunities interest you.

STEP 02

Initial conversation

Our team reviews your request and may contact you to understand your circumstances.

STEP 03

Explore a suitable option

We work with an appropriate participating partner to discuss a suitable contribution and benefits.

STEP 04

Choose whether to join

You receive the proposed terms before deciding. There is no obligation to accept.
