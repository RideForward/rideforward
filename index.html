How the process works

Every application is handled through our team—not submitted directly to the donor.

01 / APPLY

Submit your details

Tell us about yourself, your transport needs, and why this motorcycle would help.

02 / REVIEW

Initial interview

Our team reviews your information and arranges an initial interview.

03 / PREPARE

Application support

We help you prepare a clear cover letter and complete your application.

04 / DONOR

Donor decision

We forward eligible applications. The donor chooses the recipient.

Application pilot

Tell us about your situation

Complete this form to request an interview. Your application will be sent to our application spreadsheet. No payment is taken when you submit this form.

Application processing fee: LKR 750
The processing fee is payable only after your interview request has been accepted by RideForward. No payment is required to submit your application. If your request is accepted, we will contact you with the next steps and payment instructions. The fee covers application review, an initial interview, cover-letter assistance, and submission to the donor. It is not a payment for the motorcycle and does not guarantee selection.

When is the LKR 750 fee paid?
Submit your application first. If your interview request is accepted, RideForward will contact you with the next steps and payment instructions. No fee is due before acceptance. Paying the processing fee does not guarantee that you will receive the motorcycle.
